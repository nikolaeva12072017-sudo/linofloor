Natural materials
What is linoleum

A natural flooring with a century of heritage

Linoleum is a resilient flooring material made from 100% natural raw materials: linseed oil, pine resin, cork powder, wood flour, and mineral pigments — all pressed onto a jute backing. First introduced in the 1860s, it has stood the test of time thanks to its exceptional durability and eco-friendly composition.

Unlike synthetic alternatives, the colour in linoleum runs through the entire thickness of the material — not just on the surface. This means minor scratches or scuffs do not reveal wear patterns, keeping floors looking pristine for decades.

With a lifespan of up to 40 years when properly maintained, linoleum is one of the most cost-effective flooring investments for any commercial space.

Why linoleum

Key advantages

Linoleum combines the best of natural materials with modern manufacturing — delivering performance, sustainability and design flexibility for demanding commercial environments.

◈
Exceptional durability
Class 34 wear rating for very heavy commercial use. Scratch-resistant, scuff-resistant, and built for constant foot traffic in offices, hospitals and schools.
◉
Natural & eco-friendly
Made from biodegradable, renewable raw materials. Naturally low VOC emissions — certified safe for indoor air quality. A responsible choice for any environment.
◇
Naturally antibacterial
Linseed oil in linoleum creates an inherently antibacterial surface — ideal for healthcare facilities, laboratories and food-service environments.
◈
Acoustic comfort
Reduces impact sound and absorbs noise underfoot. Makes spaces quieter and more comfortable — particularly valuable in open offices and educational facilities.
◉
Easy maintenance
PUR-protected surface requires only damp mopping for daily upkeep. No harsh chemicals needed. Retains its appearance with minimal effort over decades of use.
◇
Antistatic properties
Naturally low in electrostatic discharge — protecting sensitive electronics and reducing dust accumulation. Essential for server rooms, labs and medical spaces.
