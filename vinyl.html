What is vinyl tile

The modern flooring revolution

Luxury Vinyl Tile (LVT) is a multi-layer flooring construction made from 100% pure PVC. Originally designed to authentically replicate the look of stone, ceramic and hardwood, LVT has evolved into one of the most sought-after commercial flooring solutions worldwide.

Unlike natural materials, LVT is completely waterproof, dimensionally stable, and impervious to humidity — making it suitable for any room, including areas where other materials would fail. The 0.7 mm wear layer with PUR coating ensures exceptional resistance to scratches, scuffs and heavy rolling loads.

With a grade level of 34 (very heavy commercial use), our dry-back vinyl tiles are formaldehyde-free, phthalate-free (DOTP softener), and certified E0 — safe for people and the environment.

Construction

5-layer engineering

Every LVT tile is built from five precisely engineered layers — each contributing to performance, durability and design.

1
PUR wear layer
Polyurethane reinforced coating provides scratch resistance, UV stability and protects the visual layer from daily wear.
2
Decorative film
High-definition printed film with MA embossing — replicates stone, wood and concrete textures with remarkable realism.
3
Glass fibre layer
Stabilises the tile dimensionally, preventing warping and ensuring flat installation over imperfect subfloors.
4
PVC core
100% pure PVC with DOTP phthalate-free softener. Dense core provides structural strength and waterproof performance.
5
Backing layer
Flexible base for dry-back glue-down installation. Ensures perfect adhesion and long-term dimensional stability.

Why choose LVT

Key advantages

LVT delivers the aesthetic appeal of premium natural materials with the practical performance of modern engineering — at a fraction of the cost.

◈
100% Waterproof
Unlike linoleum or wood, LVT is completely impervious to water. Suitable for kitchens, bathrooms, and any area where moisture is a concern.
◉
Authentic aesthetics
High-definition print with MA embossing replicates stone, marble, concrete and wood with tactile realism. Achieve luxury looks at commercial prices.
◇
Class 34 performance
Rated for very heavy commercial use. Handles rolling loads, high foot traffic and castor chairs without delamination or surface damage.
◈
Zero formaldehyde
E0 certified — no formaldehyde emissions. DOTP phthalate-free softener and pure PVC composition ensure safe indoor air quality.
◉
Easy maintenance
Simple damp mopping keeps LVT looking new. No waxing, stripping or specialist products required. Resistant to most chemical cleaning agents.
◇
Thermal compatibility
Fully compatible with underfloor heating systems. Excellent thermal conductivity (0.22 W/m·K) ensures efficient heat transfer and comfort underfoot.
